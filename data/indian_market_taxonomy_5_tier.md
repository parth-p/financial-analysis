# Indian Stock Markets: Comprehensive 5-Tier Investable Taxonomy (Mainboard & SME)

This taxonomy integrates the statutory **SEBI/NSE/BSE 4-tier framework** with an empirical **Tier-5 (Product / Niche / Investable Sub-Segment)** layer verified across the complete listed universe of **3,150 companies** in `EQUITY_L.csv` (NSE Mainboard) and `SME_EQUITY_L.csv` (NSE Emerge SME).

---

## Architecture of the 5 Tiers

1. **Level 1 — Macro-Economic Sector** (12 Macro Pillars)
2. **Level 2 — Sector** (22 Broad Industry Sectors)
3. **Level 3 — Industry** (59 Core Industries)
4. **Level 4 — Basic Industry** (197 Regulatory Baseline Buckets)
5. **Level 5 — Product / Niche / Investable Sub-Segment** (~500+ Granular Investment Segments & Themes)
